EUSTRESS ENGINE - BRAND ASSETS
==============================

Contents
--------
eustress-logo.svg / .png        Primary logo with wordmark. Use on dark backgrounds.
eustress-logo-dark.svg / .png   Inverted wordmark. Use on light or white backgrounds.
eustress-icon.svg / .png        Gear mark. Use for app icons and small spaces.
bliss-icon.svg / .png           Bliss (BLS) currency icon.

PNGs are transparent. The wordmark ships at 2080x280 and the icons at 1024x1024,
which is large enough for print at 300 DPI.

Usage
-----
Do    keep the logo's original proportions
Do    leave clear space around the logo of at least the height of the "E"
Do    use the light-background variant on white or pale surfaces
Do    use the PNG when you need the exact rendering shown on eustress.dev

Do not stretch, distort, or rotate the logo
Do not recolor the logo or replace its gradient
Do not add effects, outlines, or containers
Do not use the logo to imply endorsement or partnership

Minimum sizes
-------------
Wordmark   120 px wide on screen, 25 mm in print
Icon        24 px on screen, 8 mm in print

A note on the SVG wordmark
--------------------------
The SVG sets the wordmark in Segoe UI with fallbacks to Helvetica Neue and
Arial. On a machine without Segoe UI it falls back, so the letterforms shift
slightly. When you need the exact rendering, use the PNG.

Naming
------
Write it as "Eustress Engine" on first mention and "Eustress" after that.
