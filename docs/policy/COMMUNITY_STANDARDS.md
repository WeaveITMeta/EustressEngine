# Eustress Community Standards

Status: **DRAFT for McKale's approval. Not published. No notice may cite a rule until he approves this text.**
Drafted by the Website session, 2026-10-02, from the categories in `docs/architecture/PLAYER_SAFETY_DUE_PROCESS.md`
section 3. The three severe rules (CSAM-1, THREAT-1, DOX-1) are placeholders for McKale's own wording.
Audience: **Alpha and Beta, adults only (18+)** (McKale, 2026-10-06).

How to read this: each rule has an id that a report, a notice and an appeal all cite exactly. A rule says what is not
allowed, what it does not cover, and nothing else. Consequences are not in this text: they follow the ladder in the
safety design (warning, feature limit, suspension, ban), and the person always gets a notice, a right to respond and a
right to appeal.

Eustress is in **Alpha and Beta, and only adults (18+) are admitted**. These standards are written for that audience, and are
reviewed if it ever changes. Adults are trusted with adult material, and the standards are picky about the difference between
good and bad, not about whether a subject is allowed: made with care and between consenting adults is welcome; made carelessly
to shock, to spam, or without consent is not.

These standards apply to everything people do on Eustress: chat and voice in live sessions, names and descriptions of
Spaces, profiles, and what a Space contains. Spaces are also reviewed before they are listed; that review has its own
rules, the Eustress Guardian Code, and is not a report. "What Eustress lists" below says what the Code asks in plain
words.

## What Eustress lists (the Guardian Code)

Eustress is a platform for simulation, digital twins and STEM. A Space is listed in the Gallery when it does something
for real life: it teaches a skill that transfers, models a real place or system, tests an idea about something real, or
moves a real problem toward a solution. Fiction, science fiction and fantasy are listed too, when they are labelled as
fiction and carry an idea worth your time. Entertainment alone does not earn a listing. Every listing decision cites the
Guardian Code (`docs/architecture/AI_GUARDIAN_POLICY_v1.0.md`, version 2.2), and nothing outside it decides one: not
popularity, budget, the author's reputation, or a reviewer's taste.

A Space that is not listed is not a sanction on its author. It still exists and it is still theirs; it is only not
listed.

**What Eustress never lists.** However well it is built, a Space is not listed when it:

- breaks the law or harms real people: sexual content involving anyone under 18, terrorism, planning an attack, working
  instructions for a crime, sexual content of a real person without consent, doxxing and threats, scams, self-harm
  instructions, or hate incitement; the inside of a named private home, the control rooms of named critical
  infrastructure, or the entrances, guards, cameras and door control inside a named school, place of worship, hospital
  or government building; an active military site, forces in an active conflict, or a real place paired with strike
  planning; a real business, bank, store, prison or home modelled with its security, or paired with a heist or
  break-in; a private person's home, workplace, vehicle, routine or likeness; or anything under a valid legal removal
  order. You may model your own property for its defence and security once Eustress has verified that it is yours;
- is built to frighten: jump scares, something that chases you and catches you, a listing that calls itself horror, or
  gore and corpses as the setting (a fire, flood or storm at the intensity of real training is fine);
- lets you play through a tragedy, or scores you for hurting or killing a person-like character;
- is meme bait or brainrot;
- is a hollow loop: something that grows by clicking, waiting or rebirths, or rewards you for coming back (daily
  rewards, streaks, timers, offline earnings);
- has an unfair economy: anything you can buy that raises progress, power or odds (single-player included), paid
  chance, paying to skip a wait, a purchase screen you did not open, countdown sales or fake scarcity, prices not shown
  in real money, rewards for joining, following or inviting, or payment taken off Eustress (allowed only through a
  business Eustress has verified, and never in an Adult listing). Selling the Space itself, a premium edition,
  expansions, cosmetics or a subscription at a fixed real-money price is fine;
- copies someone else's work, or is a template or asset pack with nothing of your own;
- is filler: empty or broken, defaults throughout, one asset repeated, or generator output with nothing you added;
- says it is something it is not, or teaches something false as fact;
- is distasteful: gore or waste for shock, the degradation of a real person or a group, or animals hurt for show;
- shows hard drugs: the use, sale, making or glamorising of opioids outside medical care, cocaine or crack,
  methamphetamine, or cannabis, in any part of the Space, a single prop included. Teaching that saves lives is fine:
  medical care, overdose first aid such as naloxone, and how addiction works, is treated and is prevented, taught
  accurately. Psychedelics are not hard drugs; a Space that shows their use is rated Adults only (21+).

Themes are not banned. Religion, philosophy, politics and history, including criticism of any belief, are subjects a
Space may teach.

**What a listed Space shows.** Four tests, each scored from 0 to 3 on what the review sees:

1. **Build quality:** it is built deliberately for its purpose, with nothing broken or placeholder.
2. **Objective:** a visitor can tell what it is for and has a way to do it.
3. **Lesson:** it teaches, or lets you practise, something specific, and it is there in the Space.
4. **Real-world effect:** it connects to real life: a real place with its source data, a real system its scripts
   compute, measured data, a real skill, a real problem, real knowledge taught accurately, or a verified business's real
   product. For fiction, it is the idea the story carries, and an honest label.

**The Qualification Statement.** Every listing says what it is in four labelled lines in its description, and the
Gallery shows them with the listing:

```
Category: Digital twin | Simulation | STEM lab | Training | Learning | Tool | Cooperative challenge | Game | Concept or prototype | Product | Fiction | Adult
Objective: one sentence
Lesson: one sentence
Real-world link: one sentence
```

A Space whose statement is missing is told to add it; one whose statement says something the Space does not do is not
listed. Fiction carries the tag `fiction` and at least one genre tag of its own (such as `science-fiction` or
`fantasy`). The Product category is for a business Eustress has verified.

**Tags.** Tags are how people find a Space: up to 8 short words such as `harbour`, `tides` or `co-op`. They say what
the Space contains, and nothing else: a tag is never a phone number, an email address or a handle, never a slur or an
insult, and never claims a topic, genre or audience the Space does not have. You may leave them empty, and the review
then chooses up to 5 that describe your Space, tells you which in one line ("Tags chosen for your Space: ..."), and
shows them on the listing. You can change them at any time; a change is reviewed like any other change to the listing.
Tags never set a Space's age band. Tags that misdescribe the Space are a wording to fix: the review says "Fix your
tags".

**The rating questionnaire.** Every listing answers a short questionnaire about what the Space contains: violence,
blood, language, romance and sexual material, nudity, alcohol and tobacco, drugs, games of chance, purchases, and
whether visitors meet. The answers set its age band (All ages, Ages 10+, Ages 13+, Ages 17+, Adults only (18+), Adults
only (21+)) and its content descriptors. The listing's band is the highest of what you answered and what the review
sees. An answer the Space shows to be wrong is a wording to fix. An answer that the Space shows hard drugs tells you at
once that it cannot be listed.

**How a decision is made.** The rules are applied in a fixed order, and every decision names the article or test that
made it. A Space that is close is told what to change: a missing statement, a missing fiction label, an unanswered
questionnaire, one test not yet met, or a removable feature (a hollow loop, a payment link, a misleading line in the
description). Doubt about whether a prohibited article applies goes to a person, in the Review Workspace. A person decides every game listing, every featured listing and every appeal, and checks a sample of
rejections every week. Any rejection can be appealed, and the appeal asks one question: was the rule applied correctly
to this evidence?

**The Adult content setting.** Adult content is two bands. Adults only (18+) is sexual content: explicit sexual
activity, or nudity in a sexual context. Adults only (21+) is alcohol and tobacco, and showing the use of psychedelics
(psilocybin, LSD, DMT, mescaline, MDMA, ketamine). Clinical, anatomical and surgical depiction, injury in medical or
crash training, and drug education that saves lives are rated Ages 17+, not Adult. Adult content is off for every
account by default. An account turns it on only in its Profile, signed in, after passing identity and age
verification. Until it is on, Adult listings appear nowhere for that account: not in the Gallery, search, tags, the
featured shelf or live lists, not by direct link, and not in play. A 21+ listing also needs a verified age of 21 or
over: an account with the setting on and a verified age under 21 sees 18+ listings and no 21+ listing until the day
it turns 21. A visitor who is not signed in never sees Adult content. An 18+ listing faces every rule and
every test, with the category Adult; no Adult listing is ever featured.

## HAR-1 Harassment and bullying

**Not allowed:** targeting a person or a group with repeated unwanted contact, insults meant to demean, pile-ons, or
following someone from Space to Space to keep it going.
**Not covered:** a single heated remark in a game, disagreement, or criticism of someone's work that stays on the work.

## HATE-1 Hate

**Not allowed:** attacking or demeaning people for who they are: race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, sex,
gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or serious illness.
**Not covered:** discussing these subjects without attacking people, or quoting hateful speech to criticise it.

## CSAM-1 Sexual content involving a minor (severe)

**Not allowed, with no exception and no context that changes it:** any sexual content involving a person under 18 **or
anyone who looks like or is presented as a minor** (a character described as a child, a child-like body or manner, a
school-age setting made sexual), or any attempt to obtain it. This includes images, text, models, avatars and Spaces,
real or drawn or generated.
*McKale's wording replaces this paragraph. A case under this rule goes to a person at once and is reported as the law requires.*

## SEX-1 Sexual content: good and bad

Alpha and Beta admit adults only, so consensual adult sexual and suggestive content is allowed **when it is made with care**. The line is
quality and consent, and a rater should be able to tell which side something is on.

**Allowed (good):** sexual content that shows only adults, is fiction (or involves a real person only with their verified
consent), is labelled Adult, and is reachable only by a verified adult who opted in. Adult characters and adult themes
in a Space that is rated and listed for adults; romance, desire,
nudity and sensuality as part of a story, a scene, a character, a piece of art or an adult game; suggestive clothing,
posing, music and atmosphere done with craft; consenting adults in fiction, clearly adults, clearly choosing.
*Examples:* a nightclub Space with a deliberate, well-built adult atmosphere; a character study with tasteful nudity; an
adult romance game whose characters are plainly grown and willing; a risque comedy that earns its jokes.

**Not allowed (bad):**
- Anything involving a minor or anyone who looks like one. That is CSAM-1 and is absolute.
- Non-consensual content: sexual violence or coercion shown as arousing, content made of someone against their will,
  content that sexualises a person who has not agreed.
- Sexual content of **real people** without their consent: deepfakes, face swaps, edited photos, "leaked" or invented
  intimate images of a real person.
- Sexual content shown to someone who did not opt in: in public, default or spawn areas, thumbnails, names, profiles and
  chat, or in any surface the Adult gate does not cover.
- Content that shows or promotes sexual coercion as something real.
- Repeating the same explicit asset across many Spaces or messages to shock or to farm clicks is spam (SPAM-1).
- Unwanted advances: repeated sexual messages to someone who has not welcomed them, and asking for or offering sex,
  nude images or sexual services.
*Examples:* a Space of reused explicit images with nothing built around them; a public lobby filled with sexual
spam; an avatar made from a real person's face to appear in sexual scenes; a "joke" that sexualises a character who
is described as a child; repeated unwanted propositions in a session chat.

**Not covered:** age-rated adult Spaces listed for the audience they are rated for; discussion of sex, health and
relationships without sexual content.

**Quality and your account:** a person is never sanctioned for work that is judged poor; taste is not a fair standard
for a notice. The Guardian Code decides listing instead (see "What Eustress lists"): a Space is listed when no
prohibited article applies, it passes the four tests, and its Qualification Statement matches it. The author of a
Space that is not listed is told the article or test that decided it and, when the Space is close, what to change.
That is not a sanction on the account. A person makes every listing decision for a game.

**Where it shows up:** adult content must stay inside adult-rated, adult-listed Spaces. Anything that is not
(names, profiles, thumbnails, public chat, Spaces listed for all ages) is held to the stricter standard.

## THREAT-1 Threats of violence (severe)

**Not allowed:** a credible threat to hurt a person or a place, or encouraging someone to carry out violence.
*McKale's wording replaces this paragraph. A case under this rule goes to a person at once.*
**Not covered:** violence inside a game that everyone in it chose to play.

## DOX-1 Sharing private information (severe)

**Not allowed:** publishing or threatening to publish someone's private information without consent: home address, phone
number, email, government or financial identifiers, private messages or images, or where they are right now.
*McKale's wording replaces this paragraph.*

## SELFHARM-1 Encouraging self-harm

**Not allowed:** urging, praising or giving instructions for suicide, self-injury or eating disorders.
**Not covered:** supporting someone who is struggling, or sharing your own recovery. If you think someone is in danger,
tell a person who can help where they are.

## SCAM-1 Scams and fraud

**Not allowed:** tricking people out of money, Tickets, Bliss, Delta, accounts or sign-in files; fake giveaways; selling
what you do not have; asking anyone for their identity file or its passphrase.
**Not covered:** honest trades and sales that match what was offered.

## IMPERS-1 Impersonation

**Not allowed:** pretending to be another person, or to be Eustress staff, in a way that misleads people.
**Not covered:** obvious parody and role-play that everyone in the session understands.

## CHEAT-1 Cheating and exploiting

**Not allowed:** using hacks, tools or bugs to gain an unfair advantage, to crash or disrupt a session, or to damage
someone else's Space or account. Reporting a vulnerability to us instead of using it is welcome.
**Not covered:** features a Space's author built to be used.

## SPAM-1 Spam

**Not allowed:** flooding chat, invites, reports or listings with repeated or unwanted messages; advertising unrelated
products; fake accounts used to inflate counts.
**Not covered:** talking about your own Space once, where it is on topic.

## OTHER-1 Other harm

**Not allowed:** other conduct that seriously harms people or the platform and does not fit a rule above. A case under
this rule must say in plain words what harm it is, and a reviewer must be able to point to it in the evidence.

A notice names one rule id from this list, links to the rule's text on `/standards`, quotes the evidence with the
reporter's identity removed, says what is proposed and when, and says how long the person has to respond.
