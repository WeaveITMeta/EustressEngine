The domain is chosen first, not last. A DomainSchema already carries the key's type, whether it is required, its validation and its export targets — so picking the domain fills in most of this panel and narrows every later choice. Binding to a source before knowing the contract is how you get a value nothing can validate.
Outbound inherits. Export targets belong to the domain, not the parameter, so one instance never quietly ships to somewhere its peers do not. The row-level toggle only decides whether this parameter participates.
